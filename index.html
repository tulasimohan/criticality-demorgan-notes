1 Executive Summary & Mission

This repository unifies and categorizes all working notes, proof attempts, counterexamples, and drafts from Obsidian, Notion, and Overleaf regarding the Criticality of DeMorgan Formulae.

The primary objective is to prove Tal’s Conjecture on the average sensitivity / decision tree depth of DeMorgan formulae under random restrictions:

Tal’s Conjecture

Let \(F\) be a DeMorgan formula of size \(L\) computing a Boolean function \(f: \{0,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}\). Under a \(p\)-random restriction \(\rho \sim \mathcal{R}_p\), does the decision tree depth of the restricted formula satisfy: \[\Pr_{\rho \sim \mathcal{R}_p} [\mathrm{DT}_{\mathrm{depth}}(F|_\rho) \ge t] \le (O(p \cdot \lambda))^t\] for some factor \(\lambda\) depending on formula structure (conjectured \(\lambda = O(\sqrt{L})\)), implying an average sensitivity / criticality bound of \(\Gamma(F) = O(p \sqrt{L})\)?
