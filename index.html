Target: Tal's Conjecture on Average Sensitivity

Criticality of DeMorgan Formulae

A unified, cross-annotated dossier consolidating all research notes, proof attempts, counterexamples, and LaTeX manuscripts from Notion, Obsidian, and Overleaf.

Conjecture Formulation

Let $F$ be a DeMorgan formula of size $L$ computing a Boolean function $f: \{0,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}$. Under a $p$-random restriction $\rho \sim \mathcal{R}_p$:

$$\Pr_{\rho \sim \mathcal{R}_p} [\mathrm{DT}_{\mathrm{depth}}(F|_\rho) \ge t] \le (O(p \cdot \lambda))^t$$

where $\lambda = O(\sqrt{L(F)})$, establishing the average sensitivity / criticality bound $\Gamma(F) = O(p \sqrt{L})$.

Counterexample Jaikumar Radhakrishnan

Asymmetric Path Collapse: $(X \vee Y) \wedge \bar{Y}$

When querying $X$ first on formula $(X \vee Y) \wedge \bar{Y}$, branch $X=0$ yields $Y \wedge \bar{Y} \equiv 0$, rendering $Y$ completely non-influential. The naive CDT branches on irrelevant variables, creating paths that break downward closure and violate Cauchy-Schwarz factorization.

JKR Repair: Prune variables with zero output influence before branch extension.
Conditioning Gap 22 Aug 2025

The $\Delta_t$ vs. $\Delta_{t-1}$ Dependence Barrier

When unravelling path probabilities inductively: $\Delta_t$ depends on $(x_{i_1}, \dots, x_{i_t})$, while $\Delta_{t-1}$ depends on $\ell_t$ and prior variables. Because the conditions share variables across restriction steps, conditional independence is destroyed, preventing product factorization.

Resolution: Abandon step-by-step induction; use non-inductive global Banyan Trunk decomposition.
